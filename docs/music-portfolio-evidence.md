## Admission gate for additional work

Before adding a track, music video, mix, production credit or artwork as a featured public item:

1. Recover the original file, canonical public release URL or owner-controlled publishing record.
2. Verify the work title, exact personal role, credited artist name, rights and independent source. Do not turn an alias match into proof of account control.
3. Check public visibility, featured-item approval and any third-party contributor or music licensing restrictions.
4. Record the evidence date, source URL, and status in portfolio/public-music.json. A private project, raw media file or unreleased track is not automatically a public portfolio item.
5. Run the existing music portfolio and full profile validation workflow. Retain source and owner acceptance as distinct gates.

## Why this exists

The public profile should demonstrate the real music/media foundation that preceded and continues alongside creative technology work, instead of listing only development demonstrations. A verifiable producer listing is the first link. Unconfirmed recordings, uploaded private assets, legal identity, contract terms and unreleased project names are deliberately excluded.

This is a human-reviewed, agent-assisted evidence and presentation workflow; it does not establish music-industry awards, partnerships, label contracts, royalty income, or previously unverified publication credits.
